# Project charter (updated from A1)

*Revised in response to A1 marker feedback (evidence base, methodology) and A2 panel feedback (timeline buffer, staged compliance, early recruitment).*

- **Project:** AI-Assisted SMS Text-Back & Follow-Up Agent for Australian Sole-Trader Trades Businesses
- **Student / pathway:** Shreekrishna Gyawali (SHEA25124) · Startup MVP

**Problem** — Sole-trader tradies miss inbound calls while on the tools; most callers who reach voicemail don't call back. Existing tools are priced for multi-staff firms and lack native AU spam/privacy compliance, leaving ~1.7M sole traders unserved.

**Value proposition** — Recover missed calls as booked work within seconds via compliant SMS, set up in five minutes on a solo budget — protecting revenue and reputation.

**Scope** — *In:* missed-call detection + instant text-back; short follow-up; consent-safe templates; one-tap pause/override; five-minute setup; English-only MVP. *Out:* full CRM, invoicing/payments, calendar scheduling, multi-language, voice AI, multi-staff routing.

**Stakeholders** — sole-trader tradie (user/buyer); end caller (experience/consent); industry mentor (feasibility); academic supervisor (rigour); regulator ACMA/OAIC (compliance boundary).

**Success metrics (SMART)** — ≥40% missed calls texted back <60s; ≥15% texted callers respond/book (4-week pilot); median setup <5 min across 5 users; zero defects in Spam Act/APP review.

**Top risks** — compliance breach (conservative trigger, ABN + opt-out, review gate); low adoption (5-min setup tested early); schedule/capacity (Week-8 buffer, staged compliance, recruitment from Week 5).
